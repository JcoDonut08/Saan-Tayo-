# Discover surface brief

- Mode: Operate.
- Audience: A phone user choosing a Metro Manila hangout alone, often while already coordinating plans.
- Job: Browse immediately or escape indecision through a short preference match.
- Primary actions: Open a place, save it, or start Smart Match.
- Proof and content: Nine dated, source-linked venue records; sourced facts remain separate from editorial fit tags.
- Constraints: No backend, live location, maps, AI, popularity data, copied venue photography, or group coordination.
- Direction: Browse-first flat-night destination slips with compact map geometry and one location-yellow decision action.
- Memorable moment: The yellow “Can’t decide?” intervention arrives after two real browse options, turning choice overload into a short guided path.
- Approved composition: User-approved Browse-First Discover with Smart Matching plan; no generated comp was requested.
- Unresolved decisions: None for this frontend phase.
